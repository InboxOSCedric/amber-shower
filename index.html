Specification

Light ring
Ø 170 × 34 mm. The outer wall comes in four finishes, switched at the top of the model: Fine ribs (160 narrow flutes, 3.3 mm apart), Round ribs (56 fully round rods, Ø 9 mm, standing side by side around the ring; their domed ends stick out 3 mm above and below it), Pleats (90 very shallow pleats, 5.9 mm apart and 0.3 mm deep, and Smooth. On Round the pleats fade in from the underside and stop just under the sharp top edge; on Pebble and Soft square they run on over the rounded edges, top and bottom.
Bathroom view
The Bathroom button hangs the shower head on a wall arm in a walk-in shower, at about 2 m, so each shape and rib finish can be judged in place. Red light and water work there too, and Ring off takes the ring off the head in the room. In this view the head is drawn 25% larger than the sizes in this list.
Shapes
Three versions of the same product, switched at the top of the model: Round (crisp: flat top, sharp upper edge on head and ring, rounded underside, housing set squarely on the head), Pebble (round, with a fully rounded ring edge and a flowing sweep into the housing) and Soft square (a rounded-square head and ring on the same round housing).
Ring lock
Slide the ring up over the head and twist it 20°. Three steel lugs on the head run into slots inside the ring, and magnets pull it home with a click. Twist back and it drops into your hand.
Magnetic mount
The soft square cannot turn, so it is held by magnets alone. Lift the ring over the head: eight magnets around the head, one in each corner and one in the middle of each side, pull it up against a stop edge and centre it. It fits in any of the four directions. To take it off, pull it straight down. There are no buttons, slots or catches.
Shower head
Ø 120 mm, smooth body that sweeps up into the housing in one short curve. The filter sits deep inside the head, which keeps the whole unit 115 mm tall. 241 easy-clean silicone nozzles
Filter change
Take the ring off and give the head a quarter-turn. It comes away with the cartridge sitting in it, so you lift the old one out and drop the new one in, without tools.
Fitting
Seat the cartridge in the head, screw the housing down over it, then screw the whole unit onto the shower arm by hand.
Filter housing
Ø 64 × 50 mm, Amber wordmark debossed 0.4 mm into the front
Connection
Ball joint with the ball hidden inside the dome, so the head still tilts. Only a short neck shows, in the same graphite as the body, with a G½ thread (½ inch, 1.27 cm), the standard size that fits any shower arm.
Finish
Anodised aluminium, bead-blasted satin, gunmetal #4B4A49
Light
12 round LED lenses, each with a 630 nm red and an 850 nm near-infrared chip
Power
Rechargeable battery in the ring, sealed to IPX7
Charging
A USB-C port on the inner wall of the ring. Take the ring off and plug in any USB-C charger. On the shower the port is covered by the head, so it is out of sight and out of the spray. No charging stand or special cable is needed.
Filter
KDF-55, coconut-shell carbon and a polyphosphate scale inhibitor, cartridge swapped every 3 months

Design intent

  • Ribbed ring. The ribs sit on the light ring only, in one of three patterns. Head and housing stay smooth.
  • Crisp or flowing. Round is crisp: ring and head share one flat top with a sharp edge, and the housing stands squarely on it. On Pebble and Soft square the ring continues the slope of the head, which sweeps up into the housing in one curve.
  • Nothing on the outside. The USB-C port sits on the inner wall of the ring, hidden by the head once the ring is on.
  • Gunmetal with the wordmark pressed into the metal instead of printed on it.
  • Three shapes are under consideration: round, pebble and soft square.

Questions for the manufacturer

  • Shape and ribs. Which of the shapes and rib patterns can you tool, and what does each do to unit cost and lead time?
  • Light output. Measured irradiance at 30 cm in mW/cm², for both wavelengths.
  • Sealing. How the ring reaches IPX7, including at the USB-C port: a waterproof-rated port, a silicone plug, or both.
  • Battery. Capacity, run time per charge, and whether it can be replaced in service.
  • Filter. Media volume and expected cartridge life at a typical flow rate.
  • Ring lock. Holding force and wear over repeated use, for the twist-lock, and the pull force of the magnets on the soft square: enough to hold the ring through a knock, light enough to pull off with wet hands.
  • Order. Minimum order quantity, sample cost and sample timing.
